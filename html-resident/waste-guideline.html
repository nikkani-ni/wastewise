Waste preparation guidelines

Sorted, secure and prepared waste gets collected and recycled properly.

Do

  1. Use properly tied garbage bags.
  2. Rinse and clean containers.
  3. Secure bottle caps and lids.
  4. Drain excess liquid from food waste.
  5. Flatten and break down cardboard.

Don’t

  1. Do not mix chemicals or hazardous waste with regular waste.
  2. Do not leave food residue inside recyclables.
  3. Do not put dripping wet waste straight into bags.
