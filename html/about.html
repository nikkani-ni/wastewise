Building Cleaner and Healthier Communities

WASTEWISE is a proposed waste pickup scheduling and tracking system designed to improve coordination between community members, local officials, waste management staff, and waste collectors.

The system aims to make pickup schedules clearer, monitor request status, and support communication about collection locations and waste readiness.

HOW IT WORKS

Better Coordination, Step by Step

A more organized process for coordinating community waste collection.

01

Request

Submit a waste pickup request with the necessary details.

02

Schedule

Pickup details and schedules are organized for the community.

03

Map Pin

The Head of Purok or Subdivision marks the exact pickup location once the waste is ready.

04

Verify

Authorized Waste Management Administrators or Staff verify waste readiness before assigning the pickup.

05

Collect

Collectors pick up assigned waste and update its collection status.

06

Track

Monitor pickup request progress and view the latest status updates.

WASTEWISE supports coordination with existing waste collection services. It does not independently provide collection vehicles or personnel.
